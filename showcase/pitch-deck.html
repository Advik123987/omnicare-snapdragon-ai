Problem Statement

The Rural Healthcare Access & Cloud Privacy Crisis

Over 70% of India's rural population lacks immediate access to medical specialists. Primary Health Centers (PHCs) and mobile medical camps operate in bandwidth-starved or completely offline environments.

Why Cloud AI Fails in Field Medicine

• High Network Latency: 1.5s to 4s round-trips make real-time stethoscopy and interactive dermoscopy impossible.
• Total Outages: In rural camps with zero 4G/5G, cloud medical apps fail completely.
• Prohibitive Recurring Costs: \$1,500+/mo for high-throughput cloud vision/LLM APIs.

The Regulatory Privacy Wall

• Under India's Digital Personal Data Protection (DPDP) Act 2023 and HIPAA, transmitting biometric photos, lesion scans, and patient records to public cloud LLMs creates severe legal liability and privacy breach risks.

Clinical & National Standards

Explainable AI (ABCD Rule) & India ABDM FHIR R4

Explainable AI: Clinical ABCD Rule

• Asymmetry (A): Centroid quadrant area ratio.
• Border (B): Compactness ratio $P^2 / 4\pi A$.
• Color (C): Pigment cluster variance.
• Diameter (D): Caliber $>6\text{ mm}$ alert.
• Grad-CAM Saliency: Visual attention heatmaps establish medical trust.

National ABDM / ABHA Interoperability

• Generates official FHIR R4 `DiagnosticReportRecord` JSON bundles.
• Conforms to India's NRCeS guidelines with LOINC & ICD-10-CM coding.
• Enables 1-click offline-to-online sync with India's Ayushman Bharat Health Accounts.
